Our Story

Powering Ghana's
Digital Future

Founded in Accra in 2019, TechNova Store was built on a simple belief: every Ghanaian deserves access to world-class technology — at fair prices, with exceptional service. We started as a small shop in Osu and have grown into Ghana's most trusted online tech retailer, serving over 12,000 customers nationwide.

Mission

Why We Exist

We exist to democratise access to technology in Ghana. For too long, premium tech products were either unavailable locally, overpriced, or sold without genuine warranties. TechNova changed that.

Our mission is to be the most customer-centric technology retailer in West Africa — delivering authentic products, honest advice, and after-sales support that actually works.

Journey

Our Milestones

2019
Founded in Accra

Opened our first physical store in Osu with a team of 3 and 50 products.

2020
Went Online

Launched our e-commerce platform during the pandemic, reaching customers across all 16 regions of Ghana.

2021
Mobile Money Integration

Became one of the first tech retailers to fully integrate MTN, Vodafone, and AirtelTigo Mobile Money payments.

2023
10,000 Customers

Celebrated serving our 10,000th customer. Expanded to 500+ products and launched our Trade-In programme.

2025
West Africa Expansion

Announced plans to expand into Nigeria, Côte d'Ivoire, and Senegal, establishing TechNova as a regional brand.
