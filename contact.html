FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Same-day delivery is available for orders placed before 12pm in Accra. Nationwide delivery takes 2–4 business days.

Do you accept Mobile Money?

Yes! We accept MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, and AirtelTigo Money, as well as all major credit and debit cards.

What is your return policy?

You can return any item within 14 days of delivery for a full refund, provided it is in original condition with packaging.

Are all products genuine?

Absolutely. All products come with official manufacturer warranties. We source directly from authorised distributors only.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, we offer instalment payment plans for orders above GH₵1,000 in partnership with selected financial institutions.

How does Trade-In work?

Submit your device details for a free valuation online. If approved, bring it in-store or ship it to us and receive immediate store credit.
